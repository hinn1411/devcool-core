## Concepts
- **Detect ≠ prevent:** without: NAT + ALB left awake all weekend, and the "guardrail" stops nothing. With: the $10 email prompts a hibernate. Prevention is the nightly hibernate (P2-T09). Read: playbook §6 Guardrails.
- **Billing lag:** without: an actual-cost alert fires hours after the spend (Budgets refreshes up to 3×/day). With: a forecast alert fires mid-month once the trend projects past $20. Read: [AWS Budgets docs](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/cost-management/latest/userguide/budgets-managing-costs.html).
- **Credits mask usage:** without: promo credits net the cost to $0, so no alert ever fires. With: the budget excludes credits and refunds and tracks real usage against $20.

## Done when
1. A monthly COST budget of $20 (credits and refunds excluded) emails at **actual $10** (warning), **actual $18** (hibernate) and **forecast > $20**.
2. After the apply, `aws budgets describe-notifications-for-budget` lists all three thresholds with the subscribers.
3. The recipients are a variable whose values aren't committed (untracked tfvars or `TF_VAR_`).
- **Out of scope:** auto-hibernate on alert (Budget actions can't stop ECS; P2-T09); Cost Anomaly Detection (P9-T10 Could); the rest of bootstrap (P2-T01).
- **Tests:** `terraform fmt -check`, `validate`, `plan` (Claude); `apply` and the describe call (user). Log the first real alert email in the journal.
- **Files:** `infra/stacks/bootstrap/{versions,providers,variables,budget,outputs}.tf`, `.gitignore` (local tfvars).
